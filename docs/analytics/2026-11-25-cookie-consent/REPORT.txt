Change
The bottom banner now offers Accept cookies, Reject, and Preferences. Accept uses
Chickzen's existing slate primary color and editorial corner treatment. Preferences
shows essential settings and an optional analytics checkbox, initially off for a
new visitor. Save persists and closes; Cancel discards unsaved changes. The small
sliders control reopens saved preferences. Keyboard focus follows opened settings
and returns to the reopen control on closing; Escape cancels preferences.

Existing consent records are retained with their original expiration. This release
does not reset or extend choices just by loading or reopening the panel.
