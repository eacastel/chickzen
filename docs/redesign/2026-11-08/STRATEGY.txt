CHICKZEN — EDITORIAL REDESIGN & FUNNEL STRATEGY
8 November 2026 | Proposal and standalone design concept

RECOMMENDATION

Prioritize qualified inquiries for Jen's strategy and messaging work. Help
visitors understand what they need, see why Jen is the right person, and take
a useful next step. Keep the minimal, warm, editorial identity, with a visible
opening CTA on desktop and mobile.

This direction follows Emilio's request to choose the most urgent goal.
Jen's revenue mix, margins and delivery capacity remain unverified; those
should determine the final flagship offer, pricing and monthly targets.

WHAT THE REVIEW FOUND

- The same-day analytics report records 235 production-host sessions in
  May–October 2026, about 39 per month. Only 14 were organic-search sessions.
  Search Console reports 13 clicks: About 7, homepage 5, blog 1. Visible queries
  include Jen's name. The sample supports a founder-led journey and targeted
  acquisition, but cannot establish conversion lift or non-brand demand.
- No purchase/inquiry outcomes were recorded. This does not mean no real
  business happened. Direct traffic is not proof that visitors are referrals.
- The homepage repeats a large logo before long sections of broad positioning.
  Its main body CTA appears late; mobile visitors must scroll or open the menu.
- The site presents 13 services, including overlapping clarity sessions and
  audits. "Let's Begin" opens a service-selection checkout with EUR 300–7,500
  prices in the Spain view. Mobile checkout lacks the desktop hover explanations.
- Jen's About page describes 20+ years of experience, Beverly Hills Chamber of
  Commerce leadership and teaching with Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses.
  This useful credibility appears after a lengthy personal origin story.
- The existing Find Your Flavor CTA leads to /the-strawberry-lemonade-effect.
  That page returned 200 but rendered only the header/footer, without a guide
  or signup form in the read-only browser audit.
- The deployed checkout JavaScript sends payment returns to /success, which
  returns 404. No payment was attempted: a broken return is established, not a
  claim that every payment fails. The earlier audit also found an invalid
  checkout canonical and sensitive query values in a blocked analytics test.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Preserve the dragonfly logo, slate-blue typography, white space, serif headings,
blush/lavender/pale blue accents and warm voice. Add interest with composition:
Jen's real portrait, asymmetric layouts, fine rules, small section numbers,
selective italics and restrained color fields. Dark slate buttons provide a
readable focal point within the existing palette.

Use one visible descriptive H1, shorter left-aligned paragraphs, comfortable
line lengths and useful details that do not depend on hover. Keep a contact CTA
in the mobile header. Respect keyboard navigation and reduced-motion settings.
Avoid invented testimonials, client metrics, stock imagery and false urgency.

The included index.html is a clickable visual concept, not a replacement app.
It uses local copies of existing public brand assets and regular links. It
contains no analytics, newsletter capture, payment flow or simulated success.
Email links open the visitor's mail app; no message is sent automatically.
Native expandable service rows demonstrate a simpler decision structure.

HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE

1. Audience, outcome, primary contact CTA, secondary services link.
2. Brief, accurately described experience and credentials.
3. Three recognizable situations and ways to work with Jen.
4. The work and outputs: positioning, language and practical priorities.
5. Jen's working style, supported by actual recommendations.
6. A useful editorial entry for visitors who are still exploring.
7. A contact invitation explaining what to share.

The concept repeats the next step after services and at the end, and keeps it
visible in the header. Before production, confirm exact role wording/dates and
add two genuine project stories: situation, Jen's contribution, deliverables and
evidenced change. Obtain permission and distinguish career relationships from
Chickzen client engagements. Do not present every logo as a current client.

SIMPLER OFFERS

Clarify your direction
For a founder or leader needing a diagnosis and priorities. Select one scoped
paid entry service from the current clarity offers after reviewing demand and
delivery effort. Show duration, preparation, deliverables, price and scheduling.
Enable direct booking once payment and fulfillment are verified.

Shape your message
For women whose business has evolved beyond their current positioning, bio or
website. Proposed core project: messaging/website voice work. Explain scope,
examples, starting investment and process. Use a short fit inquiry and a scoped
proposal. Confirm this choice against margins and Jen's preferred work.

Grow with a strategic partner
For an established business needing continuing guidance. Explain scope,
cadence, access and best-fit situations. Offer it through a conversation or as
a continuation of successful project work.

These three paths organize choices; they do not automatically remove products
or change prices. Keep useful execution services as add-ons. Standardize names
across blog, services and checkout. Explain On Brand Lab's billing period;
checkout currently does not show a cadence. Consider it as an alumni offer
before building a separate broad acquisition effort for a membership.

FUNNEL

Primary:
Referral / LinkedIn / partner / relevant search
  -> homepage, About or matching service page
  -> fit, deliverables and real evidence
  -> short inquiry
  -> conversation, proposal and agreed payment
  -> onboarding, delivery, follow-up and referral.

Ready for a defined entry offer:
Service detail -> paid booking -> verified confirmation -> preparation.
Do not require a sales call for every small, well-defined purchase.

Still exploring:
Relevant article -> repaired Find Your Flavor guide -> consented email sequence
  -> useful example -> invitation to the relevant service.

Reuse the Strawberry Lemonade idea before creating another lead magnet. Make
the practical output explicit: a short exercise to articulate what makes the
reader's business different. Provide immediate delivery and a useful thank-you
page. A four-email sequence can cover delivery/exercise, a messaging mistake,
a real example and a relevant invitation. Email-platform integration, consent
handling and actual delivery still need implementation and verification.

A fit inquiry should ask only what Jen needs: name/email, business or website,
what needs to change, timing and a broad investment range where useful.
Confirm Jen's actual process before advertising a response time, call length
or calendar availability. The concept uses the existing email contact.

ACQUISITION AND POSITIONING

Start with women founders, consultants and established experts at a transition:
launch, repositioning or greater visibility. Treat this as a working focus;
preserve an organizational path if that work contributes meaningful profit.

Use Jen's experience and network: specific LinkedIn posts, permission-based
partner introductions, business communities, workshops and client referrals.
Turn real client questions into useful articles and service pages.

Create three substantive service pages covering fit, deliverables, scope,
pricing approach and next steps. Search topics such as website messaging audits
and brand messaging for women consultants are hypotheses to validate, not
verified high-volume keywords. Connect editorial content to a relevant offer.

Paid traffic and frequent broad blogging are later investments. Current traffic
is too small for confident A/B tests. Start with five observed prospect
walkthroughs, conversations and manual follow-up.

PRIORITIES

P0 — Repair journeys and measurement.
Fix the blank guide destination; verify payment returns, receipts and onboarding;
correct checkout indexing; sanitize analytics URLs and gate tracking to
production hosts while preserving the existing GA4 property and UTM attribution.
Before scaling checkout, derive amounts from the server-side catalog and review
order metadata and recurring-service billing. A success-page visit is not proof
of payment.

P1 — Implement the focused editorial journey.
Review the concept and copy; map it into the existing Next.js/Contentful
components. Update homepage, About entry path, service overview and contact
journey. Add real project evidence. Preserve URLs or map explicit redirects,
existing analytics history and the Contentful caching fix at 73c3992. Keep
production content editable in the CMS.

P2 — Build a repeatable acquisition loop.
Complete guide/email delivery, publish service-led content, activate selected
partners and Jen's channels, and review inquiries weekly. Refine from fit,
close rate, margins and delivery capacity.

Indicative sequence: first two weeks for offer decisions and broken-path repairs;
next two to three weeks for the core journey; following month for distribution
and review. Timing depends on content, evidence and integration access. This
is sequencing guidance, not a delivery commitment.

MEASUREMENT

Primary: qualified inquiries, conversations held, proposals, clients won,
collected revenue and gross margin. Track source and offer in a simple pipeline.
Qualification means audience/problem fit, viable scope/investment and workable
timing, confirmed by Jen rather than inferred from an analytics click.

Secondary: service interest, accepted guide subscriptions, email engagement and
checkout starts. Record accepted inquiries/bookings at the receiving system and
payments from verified provider events. Email/phone/WhatsApp clicks remain
intent signals. Do not send personal or secret values to analytics.

Set goals from capacity: desired wins divided by observed qualified-inquiry
close rate gives the inquiry requirement. Collect a baseline before promising
conversion percentages or revenue gains.

Before release, verify mobile/desktop, keyboard focus, readable contrast,
first-screen CTA visibility, destinations, real lead delivery and confirmed
payment returns. Use mock/test flows, not real charges or inflated events.
